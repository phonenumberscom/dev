Commission & payouts

Simple terms, no fine-print surprises

Commission

Earn on every completed sale

Every completed initial purchase referred or sold through your integration qualifies for commission, with a structure that scales with your level of integration.

Premium numbers routinely sell for hundreds of dollars, and the strongest vanity numbers for far more, so a single sale is worth having.

Commission applies to the initial sale, not ongoing service fees. Sales to existing PhoneNumbers.com customers do not qualify. Structure varies by integration level.

Payouts

Predictable, straightforward payments

Once a sale is confirmed and clears its hold period, funds are available in your partner account and paid out by ACH transfer.

A US-based bank account and a completed W-9 are required before payouts can begin. Your dashboard shows every referral, sale and payout in one place.

Hold period and payout schedule are confirmed during onboarding.
